The Story So Far

Chapter 1

The teacher stands at the front of the classroom while the new student stands beside her, holding several books in his arms. The teacher addresses the class and asks the new student to introduce himself.

[Teacher]: “Good morning, class. We have a new transfer student joining us today. Please take a moment to introduce yourself.”

The scene shifts to the front of the classroom. The white-haired transfer student stands before the class and writes his name on the board. He then faces the class as he introduces himself.

[Taisen]: “It’s nice to meet you all. My name is Taisen Osoreru.”

The scene shifts to two female students sitting together at their desks. One of the girls turns toward the other and speaks quietly while looking toward the new transfer student.

[Girl 1]: “Hey Yui, the new transfer student looks very strange, don’t you think?”

The other girl looks toward the white-haired student as she responds.

[Girl 2]: “Yeah, he probably thinks that dyeing his hair and eyebrows white, and wearing white contacts makes him look cool or something.”

The white-haired student is now standing behind the two girls. He looks down toward them as they realize he has heard their conversation.

[Taisen]: “You know I can hear that you’re talking about me, right?”

The two girls turn toward him. They look embarrassed as they apologize.

[Girl 1]: “…We’re so sorry!”

Later, Taisen is shown returning home through a crowded area. His white hair and eyebrows stand out against the dark suits of the other passengers. He holds onto his backpack straps, a scowl deepening on his face as he feels the weight of the surrounding gazes.

[Taisen — Thought]: “It’s really frustrating how people are staring at me right now as though I come from a different world than them.”

Taisen turns his head toward the window and looks at his reflection.

[Taisen — Thought]: “But I’m so tired of constantly having to do this. My parents were right. I should have dyed my hair and eyebrows black again and worn contact lenses like I used to. Then I would look normal.”

Nearby, several men in coats stare openly at him, their expressions judgmental. Taisen finally snaps and looks back at them with a sharp glare.

[Taisen]: “I get it, I look strange. Can you stop staring at me now?”

Later, Taisen arrives at the front door of his home. He inserts his key into the lock and turns it. Stepping inside, he moves toward the lockers to put away his things.

[Taisen]: “I’m back.”

His mother, Reika, responds from elsewhere in the house.

[Reika]: “Welcome home, Taisen. How was your first day at your new school?”

[Taisen]: “It was fine.”

Reika looks toward him.

[Reika]: “Did you make any new friends?”

[Taisen]: “No, I didn’t, unfortunately.”

Reika’s expression softens into a smile as she closes her eyes.

[Reika]: “I see. Well, it’s not that bad. Once your father finishes talking to you, you’ll understand.”

Taisen looks at her with confusion.

[Taisen]: “Really? Why?”

A question mark seems to appear beside Taisen’s head as he tries to process her words. Reika reaches out, grabs his arm, and leads him further into the house.

[Taisen]: “So, Dad is home right now? How come he’s back from work so early today?”

[Reika]: “Like I said, your father will explain everything, so come.”

Taisen walks through the quiet hallways of his home, observing the stillness around him.

[Taisen — Thought]: “Our house is so empty today.”

He enters a room where his father, Hiroshi, is waiting. Hiroshi has light, wavy hair and a thin mustache and goatee. He looks at his son seriously.

[Hiroshi]: “Hello, Taisen. The reason I called you is to show you that…”

Beside him, Reika stands with her arms crossed. Suddenly, a dark, flickering, flame-like aura erupts from her hand. Taisen’s eyes widen in shock, and he reflexively brings a hand to his mouth.

[Hiroshi]: “…Magic is real!”

Taisen stands frozen, staring at the dark energy in silence.

Reika holds her hand up, showing how the dark aura emanates from the tips of her fingers.

[Reika]: “We can release magic aura from underneath our nails. Your eyes and ears don’t deceive you, dear. Magic is real! Let my fire touch you. Don’t worry, it won’t burn you.”

Taisen looks down at his own hands, his mind racing.

[Taisen — Thought]: “My parents can use magic! Can I use magic too? What about Shinzo?”

Hiroshi watches him closely.

[Hiroshi]: “By now, you’ve likely realized that both you and your older brother possess magic abilities. We’ve already awakened Shinzo’s powers. Would you like us to unlock your dormant powers as well?”

Taisen responds without hesitation.

[Taisen]: “Yes, of course!”

He reaches out, extending a finger toward the dark flame hovering over Reika’s hand. As he makes contact with the energy, his expression remains focused.

[Taisen — Thought]: “It’s warm, but it doesn’t burn.”

Hiroshi reaches out both of his hands toward Taisen.

[Hiroshi]: “Alright then. Allow me to touch your hand once more.”

Hiroshi grips Taisen’s hand firmly. A dark, smoke-like aura flows from Hiroshi’s fingers into Taisen’s hand.

[Hiroshi]: “Your magical ability seems to be turning things into ice.”

Taisen’s eyes widen in realization.

[Hiroshi]: “You’ve just unleashed your magic power unconsciously. Let’s see if you can harness that power at will now.”

Hiroshi reaches into a pocket and pulls out a piece of fruit.

[Hiroshi]: “See if you can freeze this apple I grabbed from the kitchen.”

Taisen takes the apple in his hand.

[Taisen]: “Understood, Dad. I will give it a try.”

[Hiroshi]: “Just touch it with your nails while wishing to freeze it.”

Taisen focuses, pressing his fingers against the skin of the fruit. Almost instantly, a layer of thick frost and ice spreads across the apple’s surface.

[Taisen]: “I did it!”

His expression quickly shifts to discomfort. He looks down at his hand, which is now clutching the frozen fruit.

[Taisen]: “My hand is freezing, so my power doesn’t protect me from the cold.”

Reika looks at him with concern.

[Reika]: “Taisen, is something wrong?”

Taisen stares intently at the ice-covered apple, attempting to concentrate.

[Taisen]: “…No, I’m just trying to unfreeze the apple.”

Reika explains the limitation of his ability.

[Reika]: “You can’t reverse the effects of your magic power once it’s been used, nor can you retract the magic aura you’ve released to activate it. You can only stop using it.”

Taisen looks up at his parents, his expression serious.

[Taisen]: “And why couldn’t I use this power before?”

His parents explain that people born with magic powers require someone who has already awakened their own powers to transfer a large amount of magical aura into the dormant nail beds of the person with dormant powers. This instinctively triggers their dormant powers, allowing them to immediately gain control over their abilities and use them at will.

Taisen turns slightly, looking away as he processes the explanation.

[Taisen]: “Now that I can wield my power at will, does that mean I don’t have to undergo any training to master it?”

He pauses before asking another question.

[Taisen]: “Then how did the first magic user manage to awaken their powers?”

Hiroshi looks at him directly.

[Hiroshi]: “We don’t know for sure, but it is believed that the first magic user is the creator of the universe!”

Reika raises a finger, pointing toward him.

[Reika]: “Right now, you can only use your power on things you touch.”

Suddenly, a dark, flickering flame manifests in Taisen’s palm, startling him.

[Reika]: “But as you can see, that restriction doesn’t apply to me!”

Reika continues explaining.

[Reika]: “When you want to use your power on something without touching it, you have to expand your aura and guide it to the target with your mind.”

Taisen brings his hands together as he listens intently.

[Taisen]: “Oh, I see.”

[Reika]: “Tomorrow, we’ll teach you how to do this.”

Taisen lets out a relieved laugh and rubs the back of his head while still holding the now-thawed apple.

[Taisen]: “Also, Mom, thank you for melting the ice. It was freezing my hand.”

Reika smiles.

[Reika]: “You’re welcome, dear.”

Taisen’s expression becomes more somber as he looks toward the floor.

[Taisen]: “You still haven’t told me why we have magic power.”

Hiroshi begins to answer.

[Hiroshi]: “The reason why we have magic powers is because…”

His parents stand together against a plain wall. Reika, standing barefoot, leans her head affectionately onto Hiroshi’s shoulder. Hiroshi stands with his arms crossed, a look of pride on his face.

[Hiroshi]: “…We are royals from a parallel world!”

Chapter 2

Taisen stares at the scene before him, his eyes wide with a mixture of shock and realization.

[Taisen]: “Then that means our family is really important, right?”

He raises his hand as if trying to grasp the enormity of the situation. He then falls into stunned silence, his gaze fixed on nothing in particular as he processes the truth.

[Reika]: “Yeah, I guess you could say that.”

She gives him a gentle, knowing smile.

Taisen looks at her, his expression turning serious.

[Taisen]: “Why did you hide all this from me for all these years? And you abandoned royal life to raise us here?”

Reika’s expression softens into one of quiet melancholy as she looks down, her arms crossed tightly.

[Reika]: “We wanted to give you and your brother a chance at a normal life here. We didn’t abandon royal life. Your uncle, who’s the current king, temporarily exiled us, but now he has ordered us to come back.”

Taisen narrows his eyes as he looks at her profile.

[Taisen]: “So, that’s why you are telling me all this now?”

Hiroshi’s voice interjects.

[Hiroshi]: “Exactly. We are going back to our world tomorrow morning.”

Taisen takes a bite of the dark apple, his gaze fixed forward as he reflects on his family’s revelation.

[Taisen — Thought]: “I always knew my family and I were different, but I never could have imagined this was the reason why.”

He stands there with his school bag slung over his shoulder.

[Taisen — Thought]: “I guess it’s now safe to assume that the reason Father, Shinzo, and I all have white hair and eyes is because we come from this parallel world.”

Reika stands before him, her expression serious.

[Reika]: “We wanted you to at least get to finish high school, just like Shinzo did. This was supposed to be your final year too. But, unfortunately, we have to obey your uncle’s orders.”

Taisen reaches down to pick up his bag from the floor.

[Taisen]: “Yeah, you’re right. I will.”

Hiroshi looks at him with a steady gaze.

[Hiroshi]: “Taisen, we understand how overwhelming it must be to learn all of this at once. So, take some rest now. A special day awaits you tomorrow.”

The following morning, Taisen’s eyes snap open.

[Reika]: “Taisen, wake up!”

Taisen sits up in bed, his hair messy from sleep. A question mark seems to appear beside him as he tries to orient himself.

Reika sits on the edge of his bed.

[Reika]: “Good morning, dear. Did you sleep well?”

Taisen offers a small, sleepy smile.

[Taisen]: “I did, but I was so excited about today that I had a hard time falling asleep.”

Hiroshi’s voice comes from across the room.

[Hiroshi]: “That’s to be expected. Today is a really special day, after all. It’s time for us to return to our world.”

Hiroshi holds up a small, dark, diamond-shaped stone.

[Hiroshi]: “With this magical stone, I can create a portal to our world by infusing it with my magic aura. Watch!”

As Taisen watches in awe, a dark, swirling vortex opens in the middle of the room.

Taisen stares at the portal in amazement as it pulses with magical energy.

[Reika]: “To return to our world, we just need to pass through this portal.”

Taisen sits on the edge of the bed and reaches down to tighten the laces on his black sneakers.

[Reika]: “As you already noticed, we left your shoes near your bed to help you get ready faster.”

Together, Taisen and Reika step into the center of the spinning void. As they cross the threshold, the room vanishes, replaced by jagged rock.

[Taisen]: “…A cave?”

Taisen looks around at the rough stone walls with a skeptical expression.

[Taisen]: “This just seems like an ordinary cave. I expected something different. Something more magical.”

Reika turns back toward him and points farther into the cavern.

[Reika]: “See, Taisen, this cave is a crossing point. The portal brings us here every time we travel from the ordinary world to ours.”

Further ahead, two figures clad in full suits of plate armor stand among the trees near the cave entrance.

[Kurayami]: “They have arrived!”

Reika leads Taisen toward the armored men.

[Reika]: “Taisen, these knights, handpicked by the king himself, were charged with guarding the cave.”

The knights approach them.

[Mori]: “Well, if it isn’t Reika! And this must be your other son, Taisen, right?”

[Reika]: “Yeah, that’s him.”

The knight looks down at the boy through his helmet.

[Mori]: “Welcome, kid. I’m your mother’s brother, so that makes me your uncle.”

Taisen raises a hand in a polite wave, a small smile appearing on his face.

[Taisen]: “Oh… it’s so nice to meet you!”

The knight looks past them.

[Mori]: “By the way, Reika, where’s Hiroshi?”

[Reika]: “He’ll be here any moment.”

Just then, Hiroshi steps out from the swirling portal behind them. He is holding Taisen’s entire bed frame and mattress high above his head with a single arm.

Taisen stares at him in surprise.

The armored uncle reaches out an open gauntleted hand toward Hiroshi.

[Mori]: “Good thinking. Hiroshi, I need you to give me the magical stone. It’s the king’s order.”

Hiroshi adjusts his hold on the bed, remaining calm despite the heavy load.

[Hiroshi]: “Sorry I took so long. As you can see, I had to carry Taisen’s bed. I didn’t want to leave anything behind.”

He reaches into his shorts pocket.

[Hiroshi]: “Yes, of course.”

Hiroshi holds the small, dark, diamond-shaped stone between his fingers and drops it into the knight’s palm.

[Hiroshi]: “Here you go.”

Taisen stands nearby, silently watching the exchange.

[Taisen]: “…”

Reika then begins teaching Taisen how to use his magic from a distance.

[Reika]: “To use your power on a target from a distance, you must extend your magic aura. Though it cannot be seen, you must mentally navigate this aura, directing it toward the target with intense focus. Maintaining awareness of the aura’s exact position is critical, especially at the moment of activation, as even a slight lapse in concentration can cause you to miss.”

Taisen listens intently. He slowly raises his right arm, extending his hand toward a point in front of him as he focuses.

[Reika]: “Pointing your hand toward the target helps with aim and shortens the distance your aura must travel. With all that in mind, try freezing the tree behind me.”

In a nearby part of the forest, a man with short, dark hair stands among the trees. He is bare-chested and holds a large, flat sword across his shoulders. A small plant with several pointed leaves is attached to the hilt of his weapon. He looks toward the side with a confident, slight grin.

Taisen remains focused, but the effort is clearly taking its toll. His face is tight with strain, and a single bead of sweat rolls down his temple. He holds both hands clenched into fists near his head, staring downward with intense concentration as he attempts to manifest his power.

Taisen stands in the woods, sweat rolling down his face from the exertion.

Chapter 3

Suddenly, the shirtless man with short dark hair appears behind him, resting his large sword on his shoulders with a smirk.

Taisen turns, startled. He quickly reaches out his hand, eyes wide with focus and a touch of desperation.

The man with the sword is relentless. He lunges at Taisen from various angles, his movements swift and powerful. In one moment, he swings the blade downward as Taisen tries to evade. In another, he drops from the trees above.

Finally, the man lands a heavy kick squarely into Taisen’s chest. The force of the blow sends the boy reeling backward, his eyes clamped shut in pain.

Taisen ends up on the forest floor, slumped against the base of a thick tree. He pants heavily, one eye squinting as he tries to recover.

He slowly pulls himself up, using the tree for support. His gaze remains fixed forward, though his expression is one of deep fatigue.

The shirtless swordsman stands a short distance away, his weapon ready. Taisen reaches out a hand toward him, his fingers splayed as he tries once more to channel his magic.

Reika watches her son’s efforts.

[Reika]: “Not bad, Taisen.”

Taisen remains sitting on the ground, his body heavy with exhaustion. He keeps one arm raised toward the sky, his eyes half-lidded.

A small, jagged structure of ice has formed on the ground near the base of a tree.

[Reika]: “You didn’t freeze the tree, but you came close.”

Nearby, a small plant-like structure is encased in ice, its leaves frozen solid. Reika walks toward the ice at the tree’s base.

[Reika]: “Don’t feel bad, dear. Even skilled magic users struggle with long-range accuracy.”

She extends her hand, and dark, flickering flames begin to emerge from her fingertips, meeting the ice and melting it away.

The man with the sword, Kurayami, walks across the terrain. He looks downward, his face set in a serious expression. He appears behind Taisen, who holds his right hand up in a tense, claw-like gesture.

[Kurayami]: “Taking your eyes off your opponent is a huge mistake.”

Kurayami moves to the side, resting his massive sword over his shoulder and glancing at his own open palm with a smirk.

[Kurayami]: “Heh. You aimed at me but froze the ground? Truly pathetic.”

Taisen looks down at the ground, his expression filled with disappointment.

[Taisen — Thought]: “Damn it… I still have a long way to go.”

Taisen lunges forward, thrusting his open hand toward Kurayami’s chest.

Kurayami glances back over his shoulder at the approaching boy. Suddenly, the two are face-to-face. Taisen’s eyes are squeezed shut as his head snaps backward.

Kurayami stands with his back to the viewer, one fist clenched in front of his muscular abdomen.

Taisen lets out a sharp cry of pain as the force of the blow sends him reeling upward.

Taisen grips his face with one hand, tears beginning to well in his eyes and run down his cheeks.

Kurayami shouts at the boy.

[Kurayami]: “That hurt a lot, didn’t it? That’s what it feels like to get hit for real.”

He continues yelling at the sobbing boy, his face contorted with effort.

[Kurayami]: “So use that anger. Fight back like you actually want to hurt me this time. Don’t hold back. Give it everything you’ve got.”

Later, Kurayami sits on the floor with his knees pulled up, resting his arms across them. His large sword is positioned vertically behind him, resting against his back. He speaks calmly to Taisen.

[Kurayami]: “I’m sorry… but everything I did was by the king’s orders. He wanted you to see it for yourself. No matter how hard you tried to hurt me, you couldn’t. That’s the difference in our strength… and speed.”

Kurayami looks directly at Taisen.

[Kurayami]: “This was never meant to humiliate you. It was meant to wake you up. If I had been trying to kill you… you wouldn’t have stood a chance.”

His expression becomes more serious.

[Kurayami]: “You are a royal, and for that alone, there will be people who want to harm you, or worse, kill you. And while it is the duty of knights like myself to protect you, there will be times when no one else is there. In those moments… you’ll need to be able to defend yourself.”

Kurayami stands up, holding his sword over his shoulder. He looks toward Taisen with a small, encouraging smile.

[Kurayami]: “You’ve got a long way to go, but you’ve got potential. The sooner you manage it, the sooner you’ll be ready to meet the king and you and your parents will stand as royalty again. Now, what do you say we go back?”

He turns around, holding the sword point-down behind his back.

[Kurayami]: “And don’t forget—your goal’s simple: break my handicap. Make me use my left arm, or my sword. Do that, and your training with me will be complete.”

Later, Kurayami walks through the doorway of the cabin, his muscular bare back facing the entrance. Inside, Reika sits on the edge of a bed, her arms wrapped around her legs and her bare feet visible.

Reika looks up toward the door, her face brightening into a wide smile.

[Reika]: “Oh, welcome back, Kurayami! How di—?”

Kurayami steps inside, his profile visible.

[Kurayami]: “Hey, Reika! I’m back.”

Reika’s expression suddenly changes. Her eyes widen and her mouth falls open as she looks past him.

Chapter 4

Taisen sits on the floor, his face battered. A dark bruise swells around his left eye, and blood drips from his nose and the corner of his mouth.

Kurayami stands over him, reaching out a hand. Wisps of dark magical flames emanate from Kurayami’s fingertips and touch the side of Taisen’s head.

Taisen raises a hand.

[Taisen]: “Hey, Mom—”

Reika stands nearby, her posture tense and her expression stern.

[Reika]: “Kurayami… What did you do? I told you to take it easy on him.”

Kurayami looks away, his face partially in shadow.

[Kurayami]: “I’m sorry, Reika. The king’s orders were clear. I wasn’t allowed to go easy on him.”

Taisen looks at his hands as the magical flames work. The bruising fades and the bleeding stops instantly.

[Taisen]: “The pain’s gone. So are the injuries.”

He brings a hand to his face, feeling the skin where the wounds had been.

[Taisen — Thought]: “Her flames can do that…? But how?”

Taisen moves to a basin and cups his hands, splashing water over his face to clean away the remaining blood.

Reika stands with her back to the room.

[Reika]: “Taisen, go wash the blood off your face.”

[Taisen]: “Yes, Mom. Right away.”

Reika turns toward Kurayami, her brow furrowed.

[Reika]: “The king ordered you to do this? Why? You didn’t train this hard with Shinzo.”

Hiroshi steps into the frame, his eyes wide and serious.

[Hiroshi]: “Big bro’s here! He went through the same training!”

His expression darkens with gravity.

[Hiroshi]: “The king has grown suspicious of the neighboring kingdoms and is preparing for war. That’s why he ordered Taisen’s return, even though he’s still not an adult yet.”

Kurayami raises both hands, palms facing outward in a defensive gesture.

[Kurayami]: “Now, don’t panic, Reika. This is only speculation on the king’s part. He’d rather be safe than sorry. You know royals have the potential to be the strongest magic users. Taisen is invaluable to the king’s army.”

Hiroshi looks to the side, a look of alarm on his face.

[Hiroshi]: “What? That’s alarming. I thought we were on good terms with the neighboring kingdoms. They’re family, after all. How did things get this bad?”

Reika speaks firmly, her voice carrying a protective edge.

[Reika]: “I’m not letting my sons go to war or be put at risk, especially not because of Seichiro’s paranoia.”

Taisen wipes his mouth with the back of his forearm, his eyes narrowing with new determination.

[Taisen — Thought]: “With so much on Uncle’s shoulders, I’d probably be paranoid too. But I’m not going, and I won’t let him send anyone in my family into war, especially not my big brother, even if he’s the king.”

Taisen sits back, looking up toward the ceiling as he reflects on the day’s revelations.

[Taisen — Thought]: “So that confirms it… Shinzo wasn’t here just for visits or anything. No, he must have been here this whole time. He wasn’t busy studying abroad like Mom said.”

He continues thinking about his brother.

[Taisen — Thought]: “When they told me I’d be seeing him again sometime this year, I thought he’d just be coming home for a visit. Maybe that’s what they meant at first… But then the king suddenly called us to join the other royals.”

Taisen’s expression softens.

[Taisen — Thought]: “I can’t wait to see him again. It’s been over a year since I last saw him, my big brother. The one who understood me better than anyone. My best… and only friend.”

He looks down at his hand and slowly curls it into a tight, resolute fist.

[Taisen — Thought]: “I have to pass this training as fast as possible. My family is counting on me, even if they won’t say it out loud because they don’t want to pressure me.”

At the entrance to the cave, the armored knight named Mori stands with his back turned as a figure approaches.

[Hiroshi]: “I’m back, Mori. Sorry to keep you waiting.”

Hiroshi raises a hand in greeting.

[Mori]: “Oh, welcome back, Hiroshi.”

Mori does not turn around.

[Mori]: “I’m so grateful you decided to cover my son’s shift and keep me company.”

Hiroshi walks closer to his old friend.

[Hiroshi]: “Of course. Now, are there many people foolish enough to try and pass through here?”

[Mori]: “No. But you know my duty is just as much to prevent potential invaders as it is to defend against them.”

Hiroshi gives him a broad, friendly smile.

[Hiroshi]: “Then you can let loose and take off your helmet. It’s been so long since I last got to see your face.”

Mori reaches up with his metal-clad hands and lifts the heavy helmet from his head, revealing a face with short dark hair and a neat goatee.

[Hiroshi]: “Must feel nice to finally take your helmet off, doesn’t it?”

[Mori]: “You know it does.”

Suddenly, the atmosphere shifts. Hiroshi points a finger directly at Mori’s face, his eyes narrowing into a sharp, predatory look. Jagged bolts of white electricity crackle from his fingertip, dancing between them.

[Hiroshi]: “Now that you’ve taken off your helmet, forged with magic-negating dust, there’s nothing protecting you from me. I could electrocute you in an instant, blow your head clean off, take back the stone, and go after the king next.”

Hiroshi maintains his intense gaze, the lightning still sparking between them.

Mori stares back, a drop of sweat forming on his brow.

[Mori]: “Heh… that’s a pretty terrifying image you just planted in my head. Quite the joke.”

Hiroshi smiles.

[Hiroshi]: “Hey… I made you chuckle, didn’t I? Just like old times.”

The tension breaks as the magic fades.

Mori looks away with a scoff.

[Mori]: “That wasn’t a chuckle, you asshole. It was a nervous reaction. No wonder the king exiled you.”

The two men face each other, the animosity vanishing. Mori extends an armored hand, and Hiroshi grasps it firmly in a bare-handed handshake.

[Mori]: “Welcome back, old friend.”

[Hiroshi]: “It feels good to be back for good.”

Both men close their eyes and let out a genuine laugh.

[Mori and Hiroshi]: “Hahaha…”

Back at the training area, Kurayami stands beside Taisen. He places a steady hand on the boy’s shoulder, sensing his focus.

Taisen stands straight, his hands clenched into tight fists at his sides.

From nearby, Reika calls out.

[Reika]: “Kurayami, where did you put your sword? Don’t just leave it wherever you want.”

Kurayami does not look away from Taisen.

[Kurayami]: “I’m sure you miss your brother a lot, and I understand that completely. But I know you can get through this training quickly. I can see that new determination and confidence in your eyes.”

Taisen remains silent, staring forward with resolve.

[Kurayami]: “I believe in you. You can do this.”

Kurayami continues.

[Kurayami]: “And don’t forget, you’re going to meet and get to know so many of your relatives here. I hope you’re looking forward to that too.”

Weeks later, Kurayami stands with his back to the clearing, his muscular frame silhouetted against the light. His right arm is raised high, while his left hand grips the hilt of the massive sword resting across his shoulders.

He turns his head slightly, his sharp gaze fixed on his opponent. His right fist is held ready near his chin.

Across from him, Taisen is also shirtless now, his body having grown leaner and more defined from the weeks of training. He crouches low to the ground, one hand pressed against the earth for balance, his eyes narrowed with lethal intent.

In a sudden blur of motion, Taisen lunges forward, reaching out with splayed fingers. He moves with a speed he hadn’t possessed before, closing the gap between them in an instant.

Kurayami does not flinch, standing his ground as the younger royal sweeps past him. Kurayami brings a hand up to his chest, his expression unreadable as he watches Taisen recover from his low-profile assault.

The battle continues with a new level of ferocity.

Taisen throws a powerful punch, his forearm tensing as he aims for an opening. Kurayami moves to block, but Taisen is faster.

With a solid thud, Taisen’s clenched fist buries itself into Kurayami’s hardened abdominal muscles. It is a clean, direct strike that lands with incredible force.

Taisen pulls back and stands tall and proud. His chest heaves with exertion, showing off his own impressive physique. He raises a flexed arm, his eyes burning with fierce, new confidence.

[Taisen]: “Finally… I got my first solid hit in.”

He stares directly at Kurayami.

[Taisen]: “Today’s the day, Kurayami. I’m going to force your hand.”
